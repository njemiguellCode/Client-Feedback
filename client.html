FAQ

How do I submit a report?

Choose the project, set the report type, write your description and select Submit Report.

How do I see the administrator's response?

Open the Discussion inside the report under My Feedback History, or check Responses From Administration.

Can I reply to a response?

Yes. Expand the report's Discussion, type your reply and select Send reply.

What does "Resolved" mean?

The administrator has marked the report as handled. Any fix note they publish appears under Published Fixes.

Where do I see published fixes?

When an administrator publishes a fix note for a report, it appears under Published Fixes and on the report card in My Feedback History.

Why is my report unanswered?

Responses are written by administrators. Check back after they review your report.

Can I delete a report or a reply?

Yes. Each report has a Delete report action, and your own replies have a Delete button. Deleted items cannot be restored.
