    FAQ

    How do I submit a report?

    Type your feedback or bug description in the box under "Submit Feedback or Bug Report" and click Submit.

    How do I see the administrator's response?

    Responses appear in your report's conversation under "Your Reports".

    Can I reply to a response?

    Yes. Type a reply in your report's reply box and click Reply. Accessible only inside the report conversation, this informs administrators of your continued feedback.

    What does "Resolved" mean?

    Administration has marked the report as handled.

    Why is my report unanswered?

    Responses are written by administrators. Check back after they review your report.

    Can I delete a report or a reply?

    Yes. Every report has a Delete report button, and your own replies have a Delete button. Deleted items cannot be restored.
